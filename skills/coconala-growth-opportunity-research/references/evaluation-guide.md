# Evaluation Guide

## Growth-First Score

Score each job or market signal from 1 to 5 on these axes. Use the score to decide priority, but explain the judgment in plain Japanese rather than showing a mechanical total unless useful.

| Axis | 5 | 3 | 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Beginner fit | Explicitly beginner-friendly, small-scope, or proof-building | Not beginner-focused but no hard barrier | Experienced writers or consultants only |
| Weekend fit | Batch work, asynchronous, no weekday daytime need | Some weekday communication may be needed | Daily, urgent, phone-heavy, or weekday-heavy |
| Expertise leverage | Sharoushi/FP1 clearly differentiates the user | Related but not central | Generic task with little specialist value |
| Portfolio value | Published, reusable sample, review-building, or service-learning value | Uncredited but useful experience | Little reusable evidence |
| Scope clarity | Clear deliverables, fee, deadline, and responsibility boundary | Some unknowns but askable | Vague, high-risk, or open-ended |
| Sustainability | Repeatable theme and manageable load | One-off but useful | Too heavy or likely draining |

## Coconala Safety Score

Score safety separately. A strong opportunity can still become `B`, `C`, or `D` if safety is weak.

| Axis | 5 | 3 | 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Platform safety | Work and payment stay inside Coconala with clear terms | Some payment/contact questions remain | Off-platform contact/payment or pre-contract work pressure |
| Buyer/service clarity | Scope, price, deadline, revisions, and expected output are clear | Several terms need questions | Vague, broad, or likely to expand |
| Professional boundary | Writing/explanation/research role is clear | Responsibility needs clarification | Legal/labor/financial outcome responsibility is pushed to the user |
| Credential/reputation safety | Qualification use is accurate and modest | Marketing use needs review | The user's credential may endorse dubious or exaggerated claims |
| Public consistency | Buyer/company/profile information looks coherent | New or thin profile with no clear red flags | Inconsistent identity, bad reviews, or unverifiable business |

## Market-Signal Score

Use this when looking at existing services, rankings, blogs, or know-how pages rather than direct jobs.

| Axis | 5 | 3 | 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Buyer pain clarity | The pain is specific and repeated across services or posts | Pain is visible but broad | Need is unclear |
| User differentiation | Sharoushi/FP1 gives a natural angle | Some relation to money/labor | Generic and crowded |
| Starter-offer fit | Can become a small text/asynchronous service | Needs medium-touch support | Requires heavy consultation or ongoing operations |
| Trust-building value | Can produce reviews, blog topics, examples, or FAQ assets | Some learning value | Little proof value |
| Risk level | Can be framed with safe boundaries | Needs careful disclaimers | High legal, tax, investment, or labor risk |

## Priority Labels

- `A`: 応募・対応推奨. Strong growth fit and no major safety concerns.
- `B`: 条件確認後に応募・対応. Good candidate, but ask clarifying questions before applying, accepting, or revising a service.
- `C`: 情報収集用. Useful market signal, but not a priority now.
- `D`: 見送り推奨. Safety, scope, legal, tax, schedule, or reputation risk is too high.
- `除外`: Strong mismatch, expired listing, exploitative condition, hard experience barrier, or clearly irrelevant.

## Risk Signals

- `即日大量納品`, `毎日対応`, `平日日中に連絡必須`, `電話必須`, `経験者のみ`, `完全成果報酬`, `無料テスト`, `外部連絡必須`, `LINEで詳細`, `契約前作業`.
- Outcome guarantees around subsidies, benefits, taxes, investments, unemployment insurance, injury/sickness allowance, or labor disputes.
- Retirement-related work that asks for退職代行, employer negotiation, benefit eligibility guarantees, form submission代行, or urgent same-day labor-dispute handling.
- Requests for application filing, agency work, individualized legal/labor/tax/investment conclusions, or final professional responsibility without a clear role.
- Services that imply the user's qualification will be used as an endorsement, supervisor label, or sales guarantee.
- Extremely broad consultation scope at very low price, unlimited revisions, unlimited chat, or unclear cancellation/refund terms.

## Pre-Application and Pre-Service Questions

For A/B candidates, include 3 to 6 concrete checks:

- ココナラ上で契約・決済・納品完了まで進める流れで問題ないか。
- 納品物の最終確認責任、専門的な判断の責任範囲、修正回数はどう分かれるか。
- 源泉徴収、著作権、二次利用、実績公開、著者名・監修者名・資格名の表示可否はどう扱うか。
- 断定表現や成果保証に見える表現を避け、一般的な説明・記事制作の範囲で進めてよいか。
- 退職相談の場合、退職代行・会社交渉・申請代行・受給可否の断定ではなく、制度整理と相談前メモ作成の範囲で進めてよいか。
- 平日は確認中心、土日中心に作業時間を確保する進め方で問題ないか。
- 初回は小さな範囲で試し、継続可否を双方で判断する形にできるか。

## Proposal Template Shape

1. Greeting and reason for interest.
2. Relevant expertise: sharoushi/FP1 themes, not exaggerated.
3. Writing or consultation posture: accurate research, plain-language explanation, and safe boundaries.
4. Availability: weekend-focused, asynchronous, realistic turnaround.
5. First step: trial article, outline, text consultation, small review, or scoped deliverable.
6. Closing: willingness to align tone, structure, target reader, and Coconala transaction flow.
