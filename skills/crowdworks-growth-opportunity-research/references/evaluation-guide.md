# Evaluation Guide

Use this guide when ranking CrowdWorks or similar public freelance opportunities for the user.

## Growth-First Score

Score each案件 from 1 to 5 on these axes. Use the score to inform priority, but explain the judgment in plain Japanese rather than showing a mechanical total unless useful.

| Axis | 5 | 3 | 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Beginner fit | Explicitly beginner-friendly or portfolio-building | Not beginner-focused but no hard barrier | Experienced writers only |
| Weekend fit | Batch work, flexible schedule, no weekday daytime need | Some weekday communication or short deadline | Daily/urgent/weekday-heavy |
| Expertise leverage | 社労士/FP1級 clearly differentiates the user | Related but not central | Generic task with little specialist value |
| Writing portfolio value | Published/credited or strong sample-producing work | Uncredited but useful experience | Little reusable evidence |
| Scope clarity | Clear deliverables, word count, fee, deadline, revision scope | Some unknowns but askable | Vague, high-risk, or open-ended |
| Sustainability | Repeatable theme and manageable load | One-off but useful | Too heavy or likely draining |

## Employer Safety Score

Score the employer separately. A strong案件 can still become B, C, or D if employer safety is weak.

| Axis | 5 | 3 | 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Platform trust | Verified, rule check complete, positive reviews | New/few reviews but no clear red flags | Unverified, no rule check, bad reviews, or suspicious behavior |
| Payment safety | Clear escrow, fixed scope, natural payment terms | Some payment questions remain | Work before escrow, off-platform payment, or unnatural fee-setting instructions |
| Company consistency | Public company/profile details align | Minor inconsistencies or hard-to-check profile | Major inconsistencies, unverifiable identity, or misleading claims |
| Legal/tax clarity | Withholding, rights, credit, and responsibility are clear | Needs questions before applying | Avoids withholding/rights/responsibility without explanation |
| Reputation safety | Credential will be used carefully and accurately | Some marketing linkage needs review | Name/face/qualification may support dubious or overstated claims |

## Priority Labels

- `A`: 応募推奨. Strong growth fit, realistic weekend workload, and no major employer concerns.
- `B`: 条件確認後に応募. Worth considering, but ask clarifying questions before applying or accepting.
- `C`: 情報収集用. Useful market signal, but not a priority now.
- `D`: 見送り推奨. Employer safety, scope, legal/tax, schedule, or reputation risk is too high.
- `除外`: Strong mismatch, exploitative conditions, hard experience barrier, expired listing, or clearly irrelevant.

## Good Signals

- Keywords such as `未経験歓迎`, `初心者歓迎`, `継続あり`, `構成あり`, `マニュアルあり`, `専門知識歓迎`, `資格保有者歓迎`.
- Topics around labor law basics, social insurance, pensions, workplace rules, benefits, household budgets, insurance reviews, NISA/iDeCo, life planning, retirement money, employee benefits, and HR explainers.
- Deliverables such as one article, one outline, several short columns, fact-checking support, article supervision, or document review under a clear scope.
- Client accepts asynchronous communication and has concrete examples, templates, and revision boundaries.
- Trial work is paid, small, and clearly scoped.

## Risk Signals

- `即日大量納品`, `毎日対応`, `平日日中に連絡必須`, `電話対応必須`, `経験者のみ`, `実績必須`.
- Extremely low pay with high word count, repeated unpaid test writing, unclear rights, no topic specificity, or requests to copy or lightly rewrite existing content.
- Requests that require licensed professional responsibility beyond article writing, fact-checking, or supervised content creation.
- Employer profile has no identity verification, unanswered 発注ルールチェック, zero reviews, very recent registration, or inconsistent public information.
- The listing asks to uncheck source withholding, asks for off-platform communication before contract, or asks to start before escrow.
- The user would be credited by real name, face photo, qualification, supervisor title, or product recommendation in a way that could imply professional endorsement.
- The listing has inconsistent dates, unclear delivery scope, undefined revision count, or a monthly volume that conflicts with weekend-only availability.

## Automatic Downgrades

Apply these conservative downgrades unless the listing clearly resolves the issue:

- Real name, face photo, profile, qualification, or supervisor credit required: B at best; D if tied to product endorsement or dubious claims.
- Source withholding checkbox must be turned off: C at best until the reason is clear.
- Client has zero reviews and no identity verification: B at best for highly relevant work, otherwise C/D.
- 発注ルールチェック未回答: B at best when other signals are strong.
- Deadline is inconsistent or already impossible: B at best until clarified.
- Monthly volume exceeds weekend capacity: C unless the client explicitly accepts a small start.
- Web writing experience is required and the user lacks it: C/D even if the topic is excellent.

## Pre-Application Questions

For every A/B candidate, include 3 to 6 concrete questions that reduce downside before the user spends time writing a proposal.

Prefer questions like:

- クラウドワークス上で仮払い完了後に作業開始する流れで問題ないでしょうか。
- 源泉徴収の有無、または源泉徴収を行わない場合の理由を確認させてください。
- 納品後に貴社側で修正される場合、専門的な内容の最終確認責任はどちらが持つ想定でしょうか。
- 制度説明や一般的な手続き整理の範囲で対応し、個別の法律・税務・投資判断は含めない理解でよいでしょうか。
- 掲載時の著者名・監修者名・資格名・顔写真・プロフィールの表示有無と使われ方を教えてください。
- 著作権、二次利用、掲載期間、実績公開の可否について教えてください。
- 修正回数、追加修正の扱い、納期調整の可否を確認させてください。
- 外部ツールを使う場合、契約後の業務連絡に限定される理解でよいでしょうか。

## Proposal Template Shape

1. Greeting and reason for interest.
2. Relevant expertise: 社労士/FP1級 themes, not exaggerated.
3. Writing posture: accurate research, plain-language explanation, citations/source checks when needed.
4. Availability: weekend-focused, asynchronous, realistic turnaround.
5. First step: trial article, outline proposal, one check pass, or one small deliverable.
6. Closing: willingness to align tone, structure, target reader, and responsibility boundaries.
